Adding Cash Drawers

ESC/POS Cash Drawers: Connect through your receipt printer. Most thermal printers have a cash drawer kick port.
USB Cash Drawers: Connect directly to a USB port. These drawers appear as USB devices in your system.
Serial Cash Drawers: Connect to a serial port (COM port on Windows, /dev/tty* on Linux). Ensure correct baud rate settings.
Network Cash Drawers: Connect via Ethernet/WiFi. Configure IP address and port settings in your router.

Troubleshooting Tips:

  • Ensure cash drawer is properly powered (usually 12V or 24V)
  • Check cable connections between drawer and printer/computer
  • Verify the drawer is not locked or jammed
  • For ESC/POS drawers, ensure the printer is working first
  • Check device permissions (especially on Linux/macOS)
